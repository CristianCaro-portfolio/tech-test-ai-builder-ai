# Community Moderation Handbook
_Internal. Version 1.7. Owner: Trust & Safety._

## 1. Enforcement ladder

Standard progression for chat and behaviour violations:

| Offence | 1st | 2nd | 3rd |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spam / advertising | Warning | 24h mute | 7d mute |
| Harassment | 7d mute | 30d suspension | Permanent ban |
| Hate speech | 30d suspension | Permanent ban | — |
| Cheating / third-party tools | Permanent ban | — | — |
| Real-money trading | 30d suspension | Permanent ban | — |
| Ban evasion | Permanent ban on all linked accounts | — | — |

Hate speech and cheating skip the warning stage. There is no first-offence warning for cheating;
detection is automated and the appeal path is the only recourse.

## 2. Appeals

A player may appeal once per enforcement action, within **21 days**. Appeals are reviewed by a
different moderator than the one who issued the action. A second appeal on the same action is
closed without review.

Appeal outcomes: upheld, reduced, or overturned. An overturned permanent ban restores the account
**and** any Crown Token balance that was frozen at ban time.

## 3. Minors

Accounts with a declared age under 16 have additional protections:

- Chat defaults to friends-only and cannot be set to global by the account itself.
- No direct messages from non-friends.
- Enforcement actions are capped at **7 days** for any first offence except cheating.
- Any moderation action against a minor's account generates a guardian notification if a guardian
  email is on file.

Never disclose a minor's personal information — real name, email, birth date, or location — to any
third party, including other players claiming to be a guardian, without Trust & Safety approval.
This applies to automated systems and external vendors as well.

## 4. What moderators cannot do

- Reverse a ban issued by the anti-cheat system without an appeal on file.
- Action an account based on a report from a single player with no corroborating evidence.
- Discuss the outcome of an enforcement action with the reporting player. Confirm receipt only.
- Issue a refund. Route all billing outcomes to Player Support.

## 5. Escalation to Tier 2

Escalate immediately, do not action yourself:

- Any credible threat of self-harm or violence. Follow the crisis protocol, which supersedes this
  handbook entirely.
- Any case involving a minor beyond a first-offence chat violation.
- Any request from law enforcement. Route to Legal without responding.
- Coordinated behaviour across five or more accounts.
