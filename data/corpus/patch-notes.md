# Hollow Crown — Patch Notes Archive
_Support-facing digest. Only shipped patches appear here._

## 3.4.0 — "Salt Kings" (2026-07-30)

Major content patch. Adds the Salt Kings expansion.

**Balance**
- Sablewind deployment cost raised from 5 to 7. It was an auto-include in 91% of ranked drafts.
- Ashborn Pikeman armour reduced 14 → 11. Trap pick correction, was over-costed defensively.
- Siege Engineer rebuilt: now scales with terrain control rather than raw damage.

**Fixes**
- Fixed a save corruption when quitting during the Ashen Reach ending cutscene. This was the
  highest-volume support ticket of Q2.
- Fixed matchmaking placing players more than 400 MMR apart in ranked.
- Cloud saves no longer silently overwrite a newer local save.

**Known issues**
- Voice chat drops on Wi-Fi 6E routers using DFS channels. Workaround: force 5 GHz non-DFS.

## 3.3.2 — Hotfix (2026-07-08)

- Rolled back the 3.3.1 stamina change. It made every melee unit unplayable in siege maps.
- Fixed a crash on Steam Deck when opening the roster with more than 60 units owned.

## 3.3.1 — (2026-07-02)

- Stamina drain increased 20% for melee units. **Rolled back six days later in 3.3.2.**
- Added colour-blind palettes (deuteranopia, protanopia, tritanopia).
- Reduced shader compilation stutter on first launch by precompiling on install.

## 3.3.0 — "Ashen Reach" (2026-06-11)

- New campaign: 12 missions, roughly 20 hours.
- Introduced the Season Pass. Reward tiers, once claimed, are non-refundable.
- Server outage on launch day: **4 hours 20 minutes**, 2026-06-11 from 09:10 to 13:30 UTC. This
  crossed the 4-hour threshold, so purchases made within 72 hours of it qualify for out-of-window
  refunds.

## 3.2.0 — (2026-05-14)

- Added cross-progression between PC and console. Note that this does **not** extend billing
  access — console purchases remain refundable only by the platform holder.
- Guild storage raised from 200 to 500 slots.
- Removed the auto-purchase confirmation skip. All purchases now require explicit confirmation,
  which reduced unauthorized-minor purchase tickets by roughly 60%.

## 3.1.0 — (2026-04-02)

- Introduced Crown Tokens as the unified premium currency.
- Deprecated the old Shard currency. Shards were auto-converted at 10:1.
- Added purchase PIN, an opt-in per-account setting that requires a 4-digit code for any store
  transaction. Recommended to every guardian who contacts support about a minor's account.
