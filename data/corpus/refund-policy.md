# Hollow Crown — Refund & Billing Policy
_Internal support reference. Version 4.2. Owner: Player Support Ops._

## 1. Standard refund window

A player may request a refund on any purchase made through the Twin Hearth Store within
**14 calendar days** of the transaction, provided total playtime since purchase is under
**2 hours**.

Both conditions must be met. A player at 13 days and 3 hours of playtime is **not** eligible under
the standard window; route them to Section 3.

## 2. Purchase types and their windows

| Purchase type | Refund window | Playtime cap | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Base game | 14 days | 2 hours | Standard window |
| Expansion (Ashen Reach, Salt Kings) | 14 days | 2 hours | Playtime counted for the expansion only |
| Cosmetic bundle | 48 hours | n/a | Non-refundable once any item in the bundle is equipped |
| Crown Tokens (currency) | 7 days | n/a | Only if the full unspent balance is intact |
| Season Pass | 14 days | n/a | Forfeited if any season reward tier has been claimed |
| Pre-order | Any time before release | n/a | Full refund, no questions |
| Gift purchase | 14 days | 2 hours | Refund goes to the **purchaser**, never the recipient |

## 3. Exceptions and escalation

Requests outside the standard window are handled case by case. Grant an out-of-window refund
without escalation when any of the following applies:

- The purchase was made within **72 hours** of a documented server outage of 4 hours or longer.
- The player was charged more than once for the same item (duplicate transaction).
- The purchase was made from an account that was compromised, and an account-recovery ticket
  exists. See the account recovery runbook for how to confirm this.
- The player is a **minor** and the purchase was unauthorized by the account holder. These require
  guardian confirmation and must be escalated to Tier 2 regardless of amount.

Anything above **250 USD** requires Tier 2 sign-off even if it meets the standard window.

## 4. Chargebacks

A chargeback filed with the player's bank results in an **immediate account suspension** pending
resolution. Do not process a manual refund for a transaction that already has an open chargeback —
this creates a double-refund and is charged back to the support team's budget.

Suspension is lifted automatically when the chargeback is withdrawn or resolved in the studio's
favour. Median resolution time is **45 days**.

## 5. What support cannot do

- Refund a purchase made on a **console storefront** (PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo). Redirect the
  player to the platform holder. Twin Hearth has no billing access to those transactions.
- Refund Crown Tokens that have been spent, partially or fully.
- Reverse a battle-pass tier claim.
- Issue a refund to a payment method other than the original one.

## 6. Response time targets

| Priority | First response | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| Standard refund | 24 hours | 5 business days |
| Out-of-window / exception | 48 hours | 10 business days |
| Chargeback-related | 72 hours | Blocked on bank |
| Minor / guardian case | 24 hours | Tier 2 owns |
